# Rules coverage

What the engine (`shared/engine`) enforces, where it deviates from the published rules, and the
test that checks it. "Variation" means a lobby setting (Variations, at the top of the room settings).

## Online play

| Rule                                     | Status                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Where / test                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A person runs out of time                | Only forced choices are made for them, as an Advanced bot would: setup placements, the roll, discards, the robber or pirate and the steal, gold, volcano dice. Road Building stops. Nothing is built, bought, traded, or played; the turn ends. | `time.ts` forcedChoice; `turn-bot.test.ts` "a person who runs out of time"                                      |
| The robber or pirate isn't moved in 15 s | Placed for the player as an Advanced bot would                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `time.ts`                                                                                                       |
| Moves sent while someone else acts       | Accepted when still legal (the engine checks the current state); only an undo proposal must match the state the player saw                                                                                                                      | `server/room.ts` NEEDS_SEEN_STATE; `turn-server.spec.ts` "a move sent after another player's move still counts" |
