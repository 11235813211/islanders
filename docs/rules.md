# Rules coverage

What the engine (`shared/engine`) enforces, where it deviates from the published rules, and the
test that checks it. "Variation" means a lobby setting (Variations, at the top of the room settings).

| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| A ship joins your settlement/city or another of your ships; roads and ships meet only at your own settlement or city                                                          | In                                                                                                                                         | `legal.ts` shipSites/roadSites                                                                  |
| Setup: the road after each settlement may be a ship                                                                                                                           | In                                                                                                                                         | `legal.ts`; `maps.spec.ts` "real game … ship in setup"                                          |
| Move one ship per turn, after rolling: not one built this turn, not beside the pirate                                                                                         | In                                                                                                                                         | `legal.ts` shipMoves                                                                            |
| Only the end ship of an open route moves (a route closed by settlements stays)                                                                                                | Variation: "Moving ships" — **default "Any route end"** (a ship beside your settlement may move); "Published rule" keeps closed routes put | `variations.test.ts` "by default a ship between two settlements may move"                       |

## Icebergs (fan variant, catancollector.com/icebergs)

| Rule                                                                                                                      | Status                                                             | Where / test                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| An iceberg is a sea tile with a number                                                                                    | In (number shown on the board)                                     | `generate.ts` icebergNumbers                   |
| When its number is rolled, a die is rolled at once for its direction; it moves one tile that way if that tile is open sea | In (blocked by land, another iceberg, the pirate, or the map edge) | `production.ts` drift; `variations.test.ts`    |
| A ship on that side sinks; a route split by it slides back toward its settlement                                          | In                                                                 | `production.ts` sinkShip; `variations.test.ts` |
| Icebergs never affect roads or buildings; the pirate can't go on one                                                      | In                                                                 | `legal.ts` robberHexes                         |
