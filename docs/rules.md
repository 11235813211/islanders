# Rules coverage

What the engine (`shared/engine`) enforces, where it deviates from the published rules, and the
test that checks it. "Variation" means a lobby setting (Variations, at the top of the room settings).

## Cartographers (house expansion)

Not a published expansion: our own. Maps with lodestone tiles (or lodestone in their random mix) play it; the built-in ones are Compass Sea (3–4) and Magnetic North (5–6).

| Relay station: 2 lodestone, 1 lumber, 1 wool; 4 per player; 0 points                                                        | In                                                                                                                                                                                           | `core.ts` BUILD_COSTS, PIECE_LIMITS                                                                                    |
| A relay station goes on an edge joined to your route, like a road, and counts toward Longest Road                           | In (it is a road with a station on it)                                                                                                                                                       | `legal.ts` relaySites; `cartographers.test.ts` "relay stations"                                                        |
| No settlement or city at either end; no two relay stations (anyone's) share a corner                                        | In, both ways: no relay beside a building, no settlement at a relay's end                                                                                                                    | `legal.ts` relaySites, settlementSites                                                                                 |
